# Day 1: The Vine

::devotional
Jesus invites us to remain in Him, not as a vague idea, but as the source of our daily life.

> Abiding is not passive.  
> It is continual dependence.

- Stay connected
- Stay prayerful
- Stay rooted
::

::scripture
John 15:1-11
::

::closer
Take a few quiet minutes to ask God to teach you what abiding looks like today.
::

# Day 2: The Branches

::devotional
Jesus does not describe us as independent trees. He calls us branches.

A branch has life only because it remains connected to the vine. In the same way, spiritual fruit does not come from striving harder in isolation. It comes from remaining with Christ.
::

::scripture
John 15:4-5
::

::reflection
Where are you most tempted to live as if you can produce spiritual fruit apart from Jesus?
::

::closer
Ask God to show you one area today where He is inviting you to depend on Him instead of forcing growth on your own.
::
