# Day 1: Close the Door

::devotional
Jesus describes prayer as something deeply personal. Not performance. Not image management. Not spiritual theater.

The secret place is where we stop trying to sound impressive and simply come before the Father who already sees us.
::

::scripture
Matthew 6:5-8
::

::reflection
Where are you tempted to perform spiritually instead of simply being honest with God?
::

::closer
Take a few quiet minutes to pray without polishing your words. Speak plainly to your Father.
::

# Day 2: Ask, Seek, Knock

::devotional
Jesus invites persistence in prayer. He does not frame prayer as cold transaction, but as relationship with a good Father.

Asking, seeking, and knocking are not signs of weak faith. They are signs that we know where help comes from.
::

::scripture
Matthew 7:7-11
::

::reflection
What is one thing you have stopped bringing to God because you feel tired, disappointed, or unsure?
::

::closer
Bring that specific thing to God again today. Ask with honesty, not pressure.
::
