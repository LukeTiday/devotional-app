# Day 1: Number Our Days

::devotional
Wisdom begins when we stop pretending life is infinite. Scripture does not teach us to fear time, but to receive it soberly.

A wise heart understands that today matters. Small decisions, repeated over time, become the shape of a life.
::

::scripture
Psalm 90:12
::

::reflection
What is one area of your life where you are living as if time does not matter?
::

::closer
Ask God to teach you to receive today with humility, gratitude, and attention.
::

# Day 2: Walk Carefully

::devotional
Paul tells believers to walk carefully, not carelessly. Wisdom is not merely knowing true things. It is learning how to live in light of what is true.

Careful walking does not mean anxious walking. It means awake, attentive, and responsive to God.
::

::scripture
Ephesians 5:15-17
::

::reflection
What would it look like to walk carefully today without becoming tense or self-protective?
::

::closer
Choose one ordinary moment today where you will slow down and ask, “Lord, what does faithfulness look like here?”
::
